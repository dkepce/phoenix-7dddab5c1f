data/tr-words.txt
-----------------
Derived from "tr_50k.txt" of the FrequencyWords project by hermitdave
(https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), the 2018 Turkish list
(built from the OpenSubtitles corpus, https://opus.nlpl.eu/OpenSubtitles2018.php).
Changes: entries that are not made only of Turkish letters were removed and the
columns are separated by a tab.

License of the content: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC-BY-SA-4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . This file stays under that license.
It is used by Phoenix to put the Turkish letters c-cedilla, g-breve, dotless i and s-cedilla back into words typed without them.
